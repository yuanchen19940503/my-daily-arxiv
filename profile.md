# Research Profile (for arXiv relevance ranking)

## One-sentence summary
I work on primordial black holes (PBHs), black-hole environments—specifically dark-matter and accretion environments—and extreme mass-ratio inspirals (EMRIs), with the strongest current interest in their dynamics, phenomenology, gravitational-wave signatures, and observational constraints. I also retain broad interests in ultralight bosons / ultralight scalar fields, stochastic and scalar-induced gravitational waves (SGWB/SIGW), black-hole superradiance, gravitational atoms and molecules, PBH population evolution, and GW science across PTA/LVK/LISA bands.

## Ranking directive: current top priorities
Treat the following as the dominant relevance signals. They should normally outrank the retained interests listed later.

1. **Primordial black holes (PBHs), broadly construed**
   - PBH formation, abundance, mass and spin distributions, population evolution, mergers, accretion, evaporation, and observational constraints.
   - PBH-driven or PBH-associated phenomenology, including GW events, SGWB/SIGW, microlensing, electromagnetic or particle signals, and cosmological or astrophysical signatures.
   - PBHs as components of, or compact objects embedded in, dark-matter or accretion environments.

2. **Dynamics of black holes in dark-matter or accretion environments**
   - Black holes, binaries, PBHs, or compact objects embedded in dark-matter spikes/minispikes/halos, particle or wave dark matter, ultralight-field configurations, gas, accretion disks, or other accretion flows.
   - Orbital evolution, dynamical friction, drag, migration, resonances, accretion-driven mass/spin evolution, environmental backreaction, disk–orbit interactions, matter-induced potentials, and relativistic environmental effects.
   - Analytical, perturbative, numerical, N-body, hydrodynamic, or GRMHD studies of these dynamics.

3. **Phenomenology of black-hole dark-matter or accretion environments**
   - Observable effects of such environments on gravitational waveforms, phase evolution/dephasing, SGWB, ringdown/QNMs, continuous waves, lensing, black-hole images/shadows, electromagnetic emission, or multimessenger signals.
   - Detectability, parameter estimation, degeneracies, environmental inference, and constraints using LISA, LVK, PTA, or other current/future observations.
   - Concrete predictions, templates, forecasts, constraints, or discriminants between environmental and vacuum signals are especially valuable.

4. **Extreme mass-ratio inspirals (EMRIs)**
   - EMRI formation, dynamics, orbital evolution, waveform modeling, self-force/perturbation methods, parameter estimation, and LISA phenomenology.
   - **Environmental EMRIs are a top-priority intersection**, especially EMRIs evolving in dark-matter spikes/halos, ultralight or wave-dark-matter configurations, gas, or accretion disks, and studies of the resulting GW dephasing, waveform features, detectability, or environmental constraints.
   - PBH-related EMRIs, including PBHs as the inspiraling object, central object, or environmental population, are exceptionally relevant.

## Cross-topic priority boosts (important)
Rank by substantive conceptual overlap, not by keyword counting alone.

- A paper that substantially combines **two** of the following three core signals should rank above a paper matching only one: **PBHs**, **black-hole-environment dynamics**, and **black-hole-environment phenomenology**.
- A paper that substantially combines **all three** should receive the strongest possible priority.
- Within otherwise comparable papers, use the ordering: **three-way intersection > two-way intersection > strong single-theme match > retained/secondary interests**.
- Give an additional major boost when an **EMRI** paper intersects black-hole-environment dynamics or phenomenology. An EMRI paper that also involves PBHs belongs at or near the very top.
- Do not award the intersection boost for incidental mentions. Each matched theme must be a meaningful part of the paper's question, method, or result.

### Examples of exceptionally high-priority combinations
- PBHs evolving in dark-matter or accretion environments, with observable GW or multimessenger consequences.
- PBH accretion or environmental dynamics used to predict or constrain PBH mass/spin evolution, mergers, or populations.
- EMRI evolution in a dark-matter spike, wave/ultralight-dark-matter environment, gas, or an accretion disk, together with waveform/dephasing predictions for LISA.
- PBHs participating in EMRIs or extreme-mass-ratio encounters, especially with environmental effects or observable GW signatures.
- A dynamical model of a black-hole environment that is carried through to concrete observables, detectability, parameter estimation, or constraints.

## Retained primary research themes (high priority, below the current top tier unless intersecting it)
- **PBH ↔ GW connection**: GW signals and SGWB sourced by PBHs; PBH interpretations of LVK events, including mass-gap and sub-solar candidates.
- **Ultralight bosons / ultralight scalar fields ↔ BH / binary systems**: black-hole superradiance, boson clouds, black-hole–boson gravitational atoms, binary-black-hole–boson gravitational molecules, and their GW phenomenology.
- **Dynamics and constraints of ultralight-boson systems**: dynamical evolution, level transitions, environmental effects, backreaction, binary effects, and observational/theoretical constraints, especially those connected to GW observables.
- **Scalar-induced gravitational waves (SIGWs)**: modeling, spectral features, non-Gaussian effects, higher-order effects, data analysis, and robust theoretical issues including gauge dependence.
- **PBH formation and abundance constraints**: enhanced curvature perturbations, spectator fields, non-Gaussianity, loop corrections, and constraints from GWs, high-energy photons or neutrinos, microlensing, and other PBH phenomenology.
- **Observational interfaces**: forecasts and constraints relevant to LVK/PTA/LISA, connecting theoretical predictions to detector sensitivity and data.

## Current / near-term directions
- **PBH population evolution through cosmic history**: realistic merger and accretion histories and reconstruction of PBH mass functions and spin distributions.
- **Relativistic accretion and environmental GW effects**: dynamics and signals associated with black-hole or PBH accretion, disks, gas, and GRMHD-informed processes.
- **Environmental EMRIs**: evolution and GW signals of EMRIs in dark-matter or accretion environments, with emphasis on waveform modifications and LISA inference.
- **Ultralight-boson–BH / binary dynamics**: formation, evolution, and GW signatures of gravitational atoms and gravitational molecules; constraints from current/future GW observations.

## Secondary / lower-priority interests
- Near-horizon black-hole physics or strong-gravity phenomenology not directly connected to PBHs, EMRIs, dark-matter/accretion environments, ultralight bosons, or GW observables.
- Broader black-hole perturbation theory or self-force work when it is not clearly linked to EMRIs, detectable GW signatures, environmental effects, or the main PBH / ultralight-boson interests.
- Generic black-hole astrophysics in stellar or galactic environments when it lacks a substantive connection to dark matter, accretion, PBHs, EMRIs, or relevant observables.

## Methods & tools I use / value
- Analytical and semi-analytical modeling of GW spectra, orbital dynamics, and environmental effects.
- Black-hole perturbation theory, waveform modeling, and EMRI-relevant methods when tied to dynamics or observables.
- SIGW calculations, including gauge issues, non-Gaussianities, and higher-order effects.
- Phenomenology linking PBH scenarios to LVK/PTA/LISA observations: event interpretation, constraints, and detectability.
- Modeling ultralight-boson clouds around black holes and binaries, including superradiant growth, dynamical evolution, and GW signatures.
- Numerical and simulation-informed modeling, including relativistic accretion / GRMHD ingredients and N-body-style PBH population evolution.

## What I consider highly relevant
- Any substantive PBH paper with new mechanisms, dynamics, constraints, population modeling, or detectable predictions—not only papers whose main observable is a GW.
- Any paper that dynamically models dark-matter or accretion effects around black holes and connects them to phenomenology.
- Environmental-EMRI papers predicting waveform changes, GW phase shifts, inference prospects, degeneracies, or LISA constraints.
- New PBH-related GW/SGWB mechanisms, constraints, or detectable predictions across PTA/LVK/LISA.
- Ultralight bosons / ultralight scalar fields coupled to black holes or binaries, especially when interpreted as a dark-matter environment or when studying superradiance, clouds, gravitational atoms/molecules, dynamics, constraints, or GW signatures.
- SIGW papers addressing PBH formation, non-Gaussianity, gauge issues, higher-order effects, or data analysis.
- PBH population studies including mass/spin evolution, mergers, or accretion.
- Ringdown, QNM, or memory-burden signatures when tied to PBHs, dark-matter/accretion environments, ultralight bosons, EMRIs, or other GW-motivated black-hole scenarios.

## Usually not relevant (deprioritize)
- Modified-gravity or dark-energy papers unless they make concrete GW-observable predictions directly connected to these interests.
- Ultralight-boson papers that are purely formal and do not connect to black holes, binaries, dark-matter environments, dynamics, constraints, or GW observables.
- Generic black-hole astrophysics without a clear connection to PBHs, dark-matter/accretion environments, EMRIs, ultralight bosons, or GW phenomenology.
- Generic accretion, dark-matter, or EMRI papers in which the relevant black-hole dynamics or phenomenology is only incidental.
